UNDER THE PIXELS

A real model.
A very small grudge.

No language model, no scripted “learning” animation. A lightweight classifier updates its weights from your movement, right here in your browser.

01

You play.

Position, movement, cooldowns, and nearby obstacles become 23 features. A sample is collected every quarter second.

02

Mochi makes a guess.

Five possible actions: left, right, up, down, or idle. Each prediction is scored against your next move before training on it.

03

The weights change.

Online gradient descent updates a softmax classifier. Adaptive Mochi uses confident predictions to lead its shots.
